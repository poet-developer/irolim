<p><strong>ADnomAD is an integrated marketing communications (IMC) campaign proposal developed for my graduation presentation in Hongik University's School of Advertising and Public Relations.</strong> The project received an <strong>Encouragement Award (장려상) in the regional preliminary round</strong>.</p>

<p>As project lead, I oversaw the overall planning, strategic direction, concept development, and storyboarding. Our proposal explored how advertising could become a participatory communication experience that people actively engage with and share.</p>

<h2>Research & Strategic Direction</h2>

<p>The project began with an analysis of consumers' negative perceptions of advertising and the changing media environment. We identified a persistent problem: even in new media settings, advertising often continued to rely on one-way communication and standardized messages.</p>

<p>We focused on <strong>early adopters in their twenties</strong> who are quick to embrace new information and ideas and share them with others. This audience became the starting point for a strategy built around active participation and the social circulation of ideas.</p>

<h2>Campaign Concept: ADnomAD</h2>

<p>Under the message <strong>“Act on new ideas,”</strong> we developed ADnomAD as a campaign that invited users to explore, participate, and spread ideas through their own actions. The concept positioned advertising as an experience that could grow through its participants.</p>

<h2>Approach → Action → Sharing</h2>

<p>The campaign was structured as an expanding journey from initial contact to participation and sharing. Each medium had a distinct role in moving that experience forward:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Approach:</strong> Introduce the campaign and invite users to discover its concept.</li>
  <li><strong>Action:</strong> Enable users to engage directly through interactive touchpoints.</li>
  <li><strong>Sharing:</strong> Encourage participants to circulate content and extend the experience to others.</li>
</ul>

<p>The proposed IMC strategy connected above-the-line advertising (ATL), web platforms, below-the-line activities (BTL), and digital and social media. NFC-based interactions, a participatory web platform, and location-based mobile experiences were proposed as ways to turn audience attention into direct engagement and content sharing.</p>
