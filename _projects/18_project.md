<h2>💫 Project Statement</h2>

<p>Inspired by the overwhelming noise of the construction site next to the room I was renting, I created music-responsive visuals in Processing and projection-mapped them onto the site. One of my early media art works, this project explored my belief that art need not be confined to the white cube: an everyday source of disruption could also become a space for artistic experimentation.</p>
